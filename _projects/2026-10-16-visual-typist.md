Visual Typist is a visual LaTeX equation editor for composing mathematical expressions with structured input, editable slots, a symbol palette, and keyboard shortcuts. The visual canvas and LaTeX source stay in sync, with matching brackets highlighted in both views. It is available as a native application for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later, with a [browser version](https://haixuan-lin.github.io/Visual-Typist/) for immediate use.
