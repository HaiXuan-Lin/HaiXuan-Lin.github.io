LocalLeaf is an independent macOS application built from Overleaf Community Edition that brings its source and visual editors, PDF viewer, log viewer, and SyncTeX to an offline workflow. Open a folder on your Mac, edit LaTeX files directly, and compile with your own MacTeX or TeX Live installation through latexmk. Files stay on your computer, with no account, network connection, Docker, or database required. The project adapts the editor for a single local user, with its own local backend and application packaging.
