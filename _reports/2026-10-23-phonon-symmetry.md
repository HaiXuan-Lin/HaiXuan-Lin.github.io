Starting from the dynamical matrix, I discussed the mechanical representation of lattice vibrations, its decomposition into irreducible representations of the little group, and the role of time-reversal symmetry and corepresentations. Using silicon along the Σ line ([110] direction) as an example, I classified the phonon modes and related their symmetry labels to longitudinal and transverse acoustic and optical vibrations.
