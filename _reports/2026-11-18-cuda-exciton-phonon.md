This report introduces exciton-phonon coupling, derives its matrix elements from electron-phonon interactions, and presents `spExPh`, a computational framework that uses CUDA and PyTorch tensor operations to accelerate their evaluation on GPUs. Applications include exciton relaxation in h-BN and the effects of spin-orbit coupling on exciton-phonon interactions in silicon.
